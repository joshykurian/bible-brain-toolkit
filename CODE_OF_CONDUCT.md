# Code of Conduct

This project exists to serve people and to help Scripture reach them in their own languages. We expect everyone who takes part
to reflect that spirit.

## Our commitment
We want participation to be welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone, whatever their background, culture, language, church
tradition, gender, age, ability or experience.

## What we expect
- Treat others with kindness, patience and respect. Assume good intent.
- Listen first. Ask questions before drawing conclusions, especially across cultures and languages.
- Give and receive feedback graciously. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information, and be especially careful with information about
  believers or seekers in sensitive regions.
- Be honest about what works and what does not, including your own mistakes.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or demeaning comments
- Personal attacks and sustained disruption
- Publishing someone's private information without consent
- Spam or promotion unrelated to the project
- Sexual or violent content

## Disagreements
Christians and others of goodwill hold different views on many matters. Keep discussions focused on the project. Please do
not use the project's spaces for denominational disputes or political campaigning.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues or contributions that do not fit this code, and may restrict participation
for repeated or serious problems. To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the contact method listed on the
repository's profile or in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions.
